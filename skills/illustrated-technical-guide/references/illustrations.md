# Illustration grammar (original diagrams)
Storyboard every meaningful figure: question, key takeaway, real inputs/outputs, components, labels, evidence and omissions. Choose grammar by mechanism: relational row diagrams for SQL joins/grouping; tensor grids for attention; timelines for concurrency; packet lifelines for networks; trees/traces for algorithms; annotated plots for benchmarks. Use original accessible SVG/HTML with a numbered caption, alt/aria description, short surrounding explanation and visible provenance. Avoid abstract input→process→output boxes when a concrete trace is possible. Verify labels, arrow semantics, units and values. Editorial styling: warm ivory, ink typography, quiet teal/coral/slate/sand accents, restrained lines and plenty of whitespace. Review at desktop/mobile sizes; screenshots without inspection are not a passed review. Source images must not be copied without rights.
