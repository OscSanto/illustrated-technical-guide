# Evidence-first research
Use primary docs, official specifications, released code, papers and controlled measurements, supplemented by secondary explanations where useful. For every material claim record ID, exact source locator, accessed date, claim text, status, and destination; cross-check contested claims. Dates and versions matter. For source-audit, inventory the **entire** reference, not a cherry-picked set. If access is unavailable, mark unverified. Keep developer-reported, third-party reported, independently reproduced, derived and illustrative results separate. Never fabricate URLs, sample sizes, benchmarks or "source verified" flags.
