How to read this guide

Facts carry a numbered citation such as [1]. Claims a reader could mistake for settled fact also carry a badge:

publishedThe vendor or official docs state it.measuredAn independent party measured it and published the data.inferredA hypothesis that fits the evidence, not confirmed.self-reportedA project reports this about itself, on its own tests.our labWe computed or ran it ourselves.
